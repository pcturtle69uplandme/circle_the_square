## 📽️ DIRECTORIAL VISION & CONCEPT OVERVIEW

A Hollywood action director would treat a corporate office mockumentary not as a quiet sitcom, but as a **high-stakes corporate conspiracy thriller**. 

### Key Directorial Pillars:
1. **The Contrast Engine**: Start with glossy, ominous corporate architecture and peaceful drone shots, then smash into frantic staff soundbites, heavy brass BRAAMS, and rapid whip-cut dialogue.
2. **Staff Soundbite Intercuts**: Weave the staff talking heads (`OPENING_S05`, `OPENING_S06`, `OPENING_S07`) as frantic "whistleblower / survivor testimonials" intercut between explosive action beats.
3. **Action Sound Design**:
   - **Ticking Clock / Heartbeat**: Ominous metallic ticking sub-bass under the establishing shots.
   - **Hollywood BRAAM Stings**: Heavy low-frequency cinematic brass drops (*Inception* / *Mission Impossible* style) on major cut points.
   - **Glitch & Whip-Pan Transitions**: Sudden digital distortion and directional slide wipes between talking head cuts.
   - **Dialogue Ducking**: Automatic music volume dipping (-12dB) during staff dialogue so soundbites hit with maximum clarity.
