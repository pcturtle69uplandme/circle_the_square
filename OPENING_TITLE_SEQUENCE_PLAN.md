## 1. 📌 Overview & Concept

The opening sequence for *Circle the Square* is designed as a continuous 30-second aerial-to-interior fly-through:
1. Starts as a high FPV drone shot over the buff-brick prow (`#D9C3A0`) and amber-glowing lantern tower (`#F2A34D`).
2. Sweeps across the Shaftesbury Road forecourt plaza.
3. Passes through the double-height glazed entrance and revolving doors into the atrium.
4. Glides past oak timber slat acoustic paneling, concrete columns, and speed gates.
5. Soars down the open-plan office desk rows.
6. Decelerates and locks onto a wide 1-point perspective shot of the Prism team posing for an official company photo.
7. Ends with a corporate photo shoot collapse: Rick and Chris goofing around, Sharon glaring unimpressed, Christina applying lipstick, Trevor staring blankly into the camera, and CEO Jan Peach exploding into screaming rage.
