CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEETS — CARTOON ONLY
=========================================

The project switched to a STYLISED COMIC / CARTOON look on 2026-08-10.

USE: cartoon model sheets in this folder (being rebuilt — see below).
DO NOT USE: anything in _photoreal-archive/.

Why the photoreal sheets were retired
-------------------------------------
Google Flow blocked every attempt to generate Jan Peach solo in close-up
using his photorealistic saved character entity — three times, including
with completely neutral wording ("sitting back in his chair, quiet
repose"). The same prompt in a stylised comic style generated instantly.
Flow's own diagnosis: "Bypassing the photorealistic character entity did
the trick."

The likely cause is likeness protection on a photoreal human face, not
content policy. Jan passed fine small-in-frame in a wide two-shot and
failed every solo close-up, which fits likeness checking rather than
subject matter.

The photoreal sheets are kept in _photoreal-archive/ because they remain
the authoritative source for each character's SPEC — age, build, wardrobe,
colour palette, behaviour notes. Read them for detail; do not feed them to
an image model as a reference.

Cartoon style anchor
--------------------
Stylised British sitcom comic art, clean bold line art, flat muted colour
palette, expressive caricature, cel-shaded, 2.39:1 widescreen crop.
Absolutely NO text, speech bubbles, captions or lettering in any panel —
storyboard_slideshow.html draws its own speech bubbles.

Company is PRISM. The building is The Triangle, Cambridge — buff stone,
curved corner, ribbon windows, glazed timber rooftop lantern, low-rise
Cambridge setting. NOT London; no Shard, no Gherkin.
