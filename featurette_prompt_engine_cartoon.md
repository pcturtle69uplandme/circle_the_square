---

## ⚠️ WHY THIS VERSION READS DIFFERENTLY FROM THE PHOTOREAL ONE

The photoreal engine attached saved Character-entity images and environment photo plates to
each shot. That approach is what got Jan Peach blocked by Flow's likeness-protection filter
repeatedly (see `HANDOVER.md` §2). The cartoon assets that exist so far — 11 character model
sheets, 3 of 12 location plates — were **not** generated as saved Flow entities; they were
generated by **describing the subject fresh in text, every time**, which is what got past the
filter. Until someone tests saving a cartoon Character entity and confirms it doesn't re-trigger
the block (`CARTOON_CAST_BIBLE.md` open items), **this engine follows the same proven method**:

- No `@CharacterName` entity tags.
- No attached character reference images.
- Every shot re-describes each character's build/hair/wardrobe **verbatim**, copied from the
  blocks below, so the look stays consistent purely through repeated wording.
- Location plates (L01–L12) are attached **where they already exist** as loose visual anchors
  for set geometry, but each shot also carries a full text description so it still works if the
  plate isn't attached or doesn't exist yet.

```
Stylised British sitcom comic art, clean bold line art, flat muted colour palette, expressive
caricature, cel-shaded. NOT photorealistic. Natural character motion, mouth movement synced to
dialogue. Absolutely NO text, NO speech bubbles, NO captions, NO labels, NO sound effects, NO
lettering of any kind anywhere in the frame. 2.39:1 widescreen intent (Flow renders 16:9 —
crop to widescreen in the edit).
```

Two standing continuity rules (from `CARTOON_CAST_BIBLE.md`):
- **No lettering in frame.** Speech bubbles/captions are added later, not generated.
- **The company is PRISM** — every lanyard, nameplate, mug and sign reads PRISM.
