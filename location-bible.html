Location 01 / Exterior

Forecourt & Main Entrance

Cold open, arrivals — the "it's just a building" establishing shot

Establishing
Real basis
Shaftesbury Road forecourt, under the glazed link bridge
Floor
Ground, exterior
Signature
Tower's amber-lit timber lantern at dusk
Adjacent to
Reception & Atrium (through the doors)
brick precast band dusk sky window glow

Atmosphere

Calm, institutional, a little too pleased with itself — the "executive" establishing shot before the comedy starts. Dusk is the strongest look: deep blue sky, the whole building glowing warm from within, the tower's glass-and-timber lantern as the single most recognisable feature.

Key features

  • Central glazed double-height link bridge, dark bronze vertical fins
  • Revolving doors dead-centre, flanked by flat accessible doors
  • 39m tower behind/right, amber-lit lantern box on top
  • Two symmetrical rows of small trees with ground uplighters

Continuity

Keep the tower's amber glow consistent in every dusk exterior. Real crest is visible on the tower brickwork in some plates — exclude or obscure it.

Atmosphere

Corporate-formal, slightly cold, deliberately impressive — "security checkpoint played as a heist." Bright and even by day; warm interior light against dark glazing at dusk reads more cinematic.

Key features

  • Row of glass paddle turnstile gates, glowing indicator lights
  • Faceted amber-toned reception desk just past the gates
  • Sculptural concrete staircase, cantilevered oak treads, glass balustrade
  • Mirrored ceiling panel over the entrance run
  • Backlit stone wall carrying the company wordmark + crest — replace for Prism

Staff Restaurant / Canteen

Atmosphere

Everyday, slightly institutional canteen energy — trays, till points, roller shutters. The ordinariness is exactly what makes Jan's meltdown land.

Key features

  • Servery: black counters, glass sneeze-guards, oak slat canopy, white subway tile, "Pay point" sign
  • Digital menu screens above the servery — real screens show the canteen's own name, replace for Prism
  • Large round reclaimed-wood communal table near the servery
  • Mezzanine above: light-oak tables, glass balustrade looking straight down into reception

Location 05 / "Jan's office"

Glass Meeting Room

Jan's office scenes — the pitch meeting, the door-closed affair beat

Stand-in Set
Real basis
Small glazed meeting room off the open floor
Floor
Same floors as Location 04
Signature
Frosted dot film + triangle decal on the glass

Atmosphere

Exposed rather than private — full glass walls mean "closing the blinds" is a deliberate, visible act, which the script already leans on. No real blinds exist on these particular rooms — add as a set-dressing addition for the shoot.

Key features

  • Full-height glass partition walls, sliding glass door, black frame
  • Frosted dot privacy pattern + triangle-motif decal accent
  • Small white table seating 4, mesh-back task chairs
  • Wall-mounted TV visible through the glass from the corridor
