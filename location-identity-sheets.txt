CIRCLE THE SQUARE — LOCATION IDENTITY & SCALE SHEETS
Companion to character-identity-sheets.txt. Built by actually looking through every relevant
photo in building-reference/ (not just the architect's text notes) so these sheets describe
what the building really looks like — real materials, real light, real signage — rather than
generic "modern office" description. Each block is a self-contained prompt spec for one
location reference image (or small set), in the same spirit as the character sheets: reuse
the wording verbatim across generations so the same "set" reads consistently shot to shot.

REAL-WORLD BASIS
The building is The Triangle Building, Cambridge Assessment's real HQ on Shaftesbury Road,
Cambridge (architect: Eric Parry Architects, completed 2018). Two five-storey blocks either
side of a central entrance plaza, linked by a glazed bridge block, with a 39m tower at the
building's "prow" facing the railway. Materials throughout: brick (cream/sand tone banded
with pale precast concrete), glass curtain walling, fair-faced grey concrete columns and
soffits internally, warm oak/timber slat joinery as an accent everywhere. Three landscaped
podium courtyards + two ground courtyards. Full detail in building-reference/the-real-triangle/
Building overview.txt.

PRODUCTION NOTE — READ BEFORE SHOOTING OR GENERATING
This is a real, currently-occupied workplace, not a set. Two things need handling before any
of this becomes background plate or image-gen reference for the shoot:
  1. Real branding is everywhere and legible in multiple reference photos — the Cambridge
     University Press & Assessment crest/wordmark on the reception wall and tower, and the
     staff restaurant's own internal name ("Triangle") on the menu screens. For a fictional
     "Prism" production this needs covering, cropping out, or digitally replacing — don't
     generate or shoot final material with the real company's name/crest legible.
  2. Several reference photos have real colleagues in them (desks, canteen, corridors).
     They're fine as private layout/lighting reference but should not be used as the basis
     for any generated background crowd — treat them as location scouting, not casting.

SHARED STYLE ANCHOR — paste into every location prompt
photoreal architectural interior/exterior photography, modern UK office building, warm
cream/sand brick and pale precast concrete, fair-faced grey concrete columns, full-height
glazing, oak/timber slat joinery accents, grey carpet tile or pale stone floor, overcast-to-
soft Northern European daylight unless a shot specifically calls for dusk/interior-lit,
restrained and documentary in tone, no lens flare, no visible readable real-world branding
or crests, 35mm-equivalent, deep focus for wide/establishing shots, shallower focus for
close/cinematic inserts

NEGATIVE (all shots)
fisheye distortion, heavy vignette, warped verticals, cartoon rendering, oversaturated
colour, visible "Cambridge University Press", "Cambridge Assessment" or university crest
signage, gibberish text on screens/signage, extreme wide-angle stretch, empty/sterile look
when the script calls for a populated office

[Location #1] — EXTERIOR: FORECOURT & MAIN ENTRANCE
================================================================
Real-world basis: the entrance plaza between the two office blocks, under the glazed link
bridge, tower rising behind on the right.

LOCATION CORE
  Function in script: cold open / establishing exterior, arrivals, the "it's just a building"
    beat before things go wrong inside
  Real basis: street-level forecourt, Shaftesbury Road frontage, Cambridge
  Floor: ground level, exterior
  Adjacent to: Reception & Atrium (directly through the revolving doors)

ATMOSPHERE & PALETTE
  Mood: calm, institutional, a little too pleased with itself — the "executive" establishing
    shot before the comedy starts
  Dominant materials: cream/sand brick with pale precast-concrete banding, dark bronze-toned
    aluminium door/window framing, pale grey paving slabs
  Palette: brick #D9C3A0, precast band #E7E2D6, glazing #2B3038 (dark bronze frame), sky
    (day) soft overcast #C9D3DA, sky (dusk) deep blue #16294A with warm interior glow #F2A34D

LIGHT
  Day: soft overcast Northern European daylight, minimal shadow, cool-neutral grade
  Dusk (preferred hero look): deep blue dusk sky, building glowing warm amber/gold from
    within through the glazing — this is the single most reusable "signature" look for the
    building and matches the tower's illuminated timber-lined lantern at the top

ARCHITECTURAL FEATURES
  - Central glazed double-height link bridge over the entrance, dark bronze vertical fins
  - Bank of revolving doors dead-center, flanked by flat accessible doors
  - Tower to the right/behind, topped by a glass-and-timber-slat lantern box that glows
    amber at night — the building's single most recognisable feature
  - Two symmetrical rows of small ornamental trees with ground uplighters leading to the door
  - Precast concrete grid facade on the flanking five-storey blocks, punched windows

SET DRESSING / PRACTICALS
  Picnic-style benches and low box-hedge planters near the tower base; no cars in the
    immediate forecourt (pedestrian plaza); occasional single figure in business dress
    walking toward the doors reads well at this scale (see reference plates)

CONTINUITY NOTES
  - Keep the tower's amber lantern glow consistent in every dusk/night exterior — it's the
    building's "signature light" the way a lighthouse beam would be
  - Real crest/wordmark is on the tower brickwork in some plates (Triangle_Tower.jpg) —
    exclude or obscure in generated stills
  - Trees are young/small (recently planted) — don't generate mature full-canopy trees
