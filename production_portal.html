Industry Screenplay (Fountain Format)

Complete script text for featurette episode "Project Inception".

TITLE: CIRCLE THE SQUARE — FEATURETTE EPISODE FORMAT: Screenplay (Fountain) INT. JAN'S OFFICE - DAY It has been a troubling few months at ExInc for CEO JAN PEACH (52, soft build, tight navy suit, comb-over). Every idea he floated appeared to fail almost immediately. He thought something must be wrong with how his stupid colleagues had implemented his suggestions. Absolutely nothing else could be the cause. Jan would deal with that later, as for now he has a meeting with CHRISTINA DROSS (38, slim, dark bob, crisp blazer). CHRISTINA Morning Jan, how are you this morning? JAN (sighing) Oh you know -- another day dealing with these morons that cannot understand the discipline and brilliance it takes to be the CEO of a place like this. CHRISTINA I see. Well I might be able to help with that. I have an idea to help communicate better with the whole department. JAN I'm listening. CHRISTINA Well, every two weeks on a Friday we do a "breakfast meeting" and offer some pastries or some other junk loaded with as much sugar as humanly possible, and we give a talk about whatever for thirty mins. We then take any questions and pretend to listen. JAN (enthused) I am liking the idea of this so far. Especially if we ensure that the sugar in the breakfast junk is just enough to pacify the workers' brains so as not to care what we are talking about, but not too much so as to result in no actual work being done afterwards. CHRISTINA Thought you would like it. Friday statistically is a less productive day anyway, so we could just up the sugar content as much as we like. JAN Great. Make it so. CHRISTINA I am sorry, what? JAN You never seen Star Trek Next Generation? CHRISTINA (heavy sarcasm) Shockingly no. JAN Fine, just make it happen. Start tomorrow with the first meeting. Now get out, I have got to prepare for a short presentation to the whole staff in a few mins. CHRISTINA Is this another one of your poorly rehearsed presentations? JAN Jesus! Everyone thinks they know better than me! I am the one with the MBA from the University of -- CHRISTINA Made Up Place? JAN (screaming, red-faced) GET OUT NOW YOU STUPID COW! JUST GET THAT DAMN MEETING ORGANISED! Christina quickly leaves, shutting the glass door calmly behind her.
